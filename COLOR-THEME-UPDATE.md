## 📊 Color Accessibility

All orange colors meet **WCAG AA standards**:

✅ Orange on white: Contrast ratio > 4.5:1  
✅ White on orange: Contrast ratio > 4.5:1  
✅ Orange on dark: Contrast ratio > 7:1  
✅ Readable for color-blind users
