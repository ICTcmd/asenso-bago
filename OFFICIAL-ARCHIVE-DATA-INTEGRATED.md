## 📝 OFFICIAL NAME ORIGINS (All from bagocity.gov.ph)

### Historical Stories:

1. **Abuanan** - Changed from "Numancia" (Spanish town name) to Abuanan by Ambrosio Abuana in 1898
2. **Alianza** - "Alliance of forces" between refugees and outlaws fleeing Spanish cruelty
3. **Atipuluan** - From "ati" (bandits) + "puloy-an" (abode) - settlers protected with bamboo fences
4. **Bacong** - Named after medicinal plant with giant onion-like rhizome used for boils
5. **Bagroy** - "Mabagrong ulo" (head would sound) - punishment expression for mistakes
6. **Binubuhan** - "Bubu" (to pour) - where priests performed baptism with river water
7. **Busay** - Named after waterfalls (now disappeared due to erosion)
8. **Calumangan** - Mispronounced "kalunangan" (muddy) by Spanish officials
9. **Caridad** - Named after hospitable woman who sheltered travelers from heat/rain
10. **Don Jorge L. Araneta** - Honors Ma-ao Sugar Milling Company founder
11. **Dulao** - "Mandulao" tree bark cured soldiers' yellow skin disease
12. **Ilijan** - No legend, once forest with wild pigs, deer, imperial doves
13. **Lag-asan** - "Place where people ran after another" - trading port robber chases
14. **Ma-ao** - "Mahao" - point where mountain people ate breakfast
15. **Mailum** - Misunderstood "Indi kami ma itom, mailum-ilum lang" (not dusky, just brownish)
16. **Malingin** - "Lingin" (dizzy) - two big whirlpools: Malingin Daku & Diutay
17. **Napoles** - Dense forest hideout for fugitives in early 1800s
18. **Pacol** - Named after large "pacol" plants dominating the area
19. **Poblacion** - City center with Torres Coliseum and Bantayan Park
20. **Sagasa** - "Sagasa" trees with dense shade for trading convergence
21. **Sampinit** - Part of Bago pueblo before 1898 revolution
22. **Tabunan** - Named after "tabon" birds that came in great numbers
23. **Taloc** - 19th century Panay immigration due to sugar industry boom
