## 💡 KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

### Why Orange #E67E22?
1. ✅ Close to Mayor's #F28C28 (recognizable)
2. ✅ Better WCAG contrast (4.73:1 on navy)
3. ✅ Works on white and dark backgrounds
4. ✅ Professional yet warm

### Why Preserve Navy?
1. ✅ Government authority and trust
2. ✅ Recent Phase 1 investment
3. ✅ Visual structure for orange to shine
4. ✅ Professional city-government appearance

### Why Microphone Icon?
1. ✅ Represents "State of the City **Address**" (speech)
2. ✅ Matches existing stroke icon style
3. ✅ More appropriate than graduation cap (academic)
4. ✅ Recognizable at small sizes
